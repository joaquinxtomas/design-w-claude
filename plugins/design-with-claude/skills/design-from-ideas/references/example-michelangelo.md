# Example: a story with type inspired by Michelangelo's paintings

Request: "A story for a painting workshop, with a typeface inspired by Michelangelo's paintings."

## Works identified
- Sistine Chapel ceiling (1508–1512): includes The Creation of Adam, prophets and sibyls.
- The Last Judgment (1536–1541), altar wall of the Sistine Chapel.
- Doni Tondo (c. 1506), Uffizi.
- Pauline Chapel frescoes (1542–1550): The Conversion of Saul, The Crucifixion of St Peter.
- Unfinished panels at the National Gallery: The Entombment, the Manchester Madonna.

There is no "Michelangelo typeface": look for letters he painted. Under each prophet and sibyl on the ceiling there are plaques with the name in Roman capitals (e.g. "DELPHICA").

## Direction

| Idea from the work | Design decision |
| --- | --- |
| Plaques naming prophets and sibyls | Roman capitals (Cinzel; Trajan on Adobe Fonts as an alternative), wide spacing, dots between words |
| Painted grisaille architecture on the ceiling | Pilasters and cornices in stone tones |
| Ultramarine blue of The Last Judgment | Central arched panel, blue with clouds |
| Fresco technique | Plaster texture, cracks, slightly worn letters |
| Renaissance humanist handwriting | Italic subhead (Cormorant Garamond) |

## Layout used (1080×1920)
- Brand name at the top, centered, Cinzel 34 px, spacing 9.
- Cornices at y = 250 and y = 1590; 96 px pilasters on the sides.
- Arched panel from x = 150 to 930, y = 420 to 1250.
- Headline: "NOCHE · DE" 58 px + "FRESCO" 150 px, cream.
- Lower plaque with two lines of inscription, in a frame with volutes.
- Call to action at the bottom, Cinzel 36 px.

Palette: plaster #ECD6B0 → #C49666, ultramarine #2F4A7A, stone #D6CAB6, ink #462F1E, cream #F4ECD8.
